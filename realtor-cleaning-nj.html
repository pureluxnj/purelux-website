Realtor & listing cleaning
in North Jersey.

Purelux Cleaning helps North NJ realtors and property professionals prepare homes for listing photos, showings, open houses, buyer walkthroughs, and market-ready presentation. WHEN THIS SERVICE FITS

Before listing photos

Clean kitchens, bathrooms, floors, mirrors, and visible surfaces can make photos feel more polished and less distracting.

Before an open house

A fresh presentation helps buyers focus on the home instead of dust, residue, fingerprints, or overlooked details.

Before a final walkthrough

A property-ready clean can support a smoother handoff before closing, rental turnover, or buyer approval.

For stale listings

If a home has been shown repeatedly, a reset can bring back freshness before the next round of showings.

Do you clean homes before listing photos?+

Yes. Purelux Cleaning provides pre-listing and pre-photo cleaning for North NJ homes that need stronger presentation before they go to market.

Yes. Realtor cleaning can help kitchens, bathrooms, floors, entries, surfaces, and detail zones feel more polished before an open house.

Do you clean vacant and occupied listings?+

Yes. We can quote vacant, staged, furnished, or occupied homes based on condition, access, scope, and timeline.
