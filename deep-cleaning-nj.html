THE WORK, EXPLAINED

A scope you
can understand.

These are service focus areas, not a universal promise. Exact inclusions depend on the property and your accepted quote.

01

Kitchen reset

Counters, sinks, cabinet fronts, exterior appliances, backsplash areas, touchpoints, floor edges, and buildup-prone kitchen detail zones.

02

Bathroom detail

Showers, tubs, toilets, vanities, fixtures, mirrors, tile edges, high-touch hardware, and areas where soap residue or mineral buildup collects.

03

Baseboards and trim

Baseboards, door frames, reachable trim, corners, and room edges receive the extra attention that separates a reset from basic maintenance.

04

Dust and surface control

Visible dust, ledges, furniture surfaces, lamps, fixtures, common touchpoints, and reachable areas are cleaned with a structured room-by-room flow.

05

Floor finishing pass

Floors are vacuumed and mopped with attention to edges, transitions, corners, and the areas that show whether the room was finished carefully.

06

Optional add-ons

Inside oven, inside fridge, inside cabinets, interior windows, basement, garage, or other extras can be quoted when they fit the service scope.

WHEN THIS SERVICE FITS

Before recurring service

Most homes that need structure begin with deep cleaning so weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly maintenance can start from a cleaner baseline.

After a busy season

If the home has fallen behind, a reset visit can bring order back to kitchens, bathrooms, floors, surfaces, and detail zones.

Before hosting or listing

Deep cleaning helps a home feel more polished before guests, photos, showings, inspections, or important family events.

For buildup and detail zones

When baseboards, fixtures, tile edges, appliances, and bathroom surfaces need extra attention, deep cleaning is the stronger fit.

BEFORE YOU REQUEST

A few useful answers.

When should I book deep cleaning instead of regular cleaning?+

Book deep cleaning when the home needs a first-visit reset, visible buildup removed, detail zones handled, or a stronger baseline before recurring maintenance.

Do you clean kitchens and bathrooms in detail?+

Yes. Deep cleaning commonly focuses on kitchens, bathrooms, sinks, fixtures, tile edges, cabinet fronts, exterior appliances, buildup points, and high-touch areas.

Are inside appliances included?+

Inside fridge, inside oven, and inside cabinets are optional add-ons. We quote those items clearly so the scope is understood before the visit.

Do I need deep cleaning before recurring cleaning?+

Many homes do. If the home needs a reset, deep cleaning helps recurring service begin from a cleaner and more consistent baseline.
