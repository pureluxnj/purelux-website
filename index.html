A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before your clean.

Do I need a deep clean before regular service?+

Not always. We review current conditions and your priorities. Essential Care may be enough, or Comfort Care can add detail in selected areas without a whole-home deep clean.

How are the care plans priced?+

You receive a fixed quote based on the agreed scope, current condition, selected extras and frequency. Review your quote before scheduling.

Where does Purelux work?+

Purelux serves North Jersey, with a focus on Bergen and Passaic Counties. Share your town to check availability for your location.

Can I request appliance interiors?+

Oven and fridge interior cleaning can be discussed as part of your requested scope. Confirm add-ons and pricing before scheduling.

Does this form book a cleaning?+

No. Your request starts a conversation about scope, pricing, and timing. A cleaning is booked only after the plan is accepted and scheduling is confirmed.

WHAT SHAPES YOUR ESTIMATE

A useful quote
starts with the whole picture.

Two homes of the same size can need very different work. These details help define the scope before a price is proposed.

01 / SPACE

The property itself

Home type, bedrooms, bathrooms, and approximate square footage define the areas to plan for.

02 / CONDITION

What needs attention

Current condition separates routine upkeep from buildup removal, a deep reset, or renovation dust.

03 / SCOPE

The extras you choose

Inside appliances, cabinets, interior windows, laundry, basement, and garage needs belong in the request.

04 / RHYTHM

Frequency & timing

One-time or recurring care, your preferred timing, and special priorities help shape the proposed plan.
