Purelux Quality Standard

The finish we look for before we call the home complete.

Premium cleaning is not louder or flashier. It is a repeatable standard in the small details that make the home feel cared for.

01

No visible dust on edges

Baseboards, corners, ledges, frames, and transitions get checked because edges reveal the quality of the clean.

02

Kitchen sinks dry and polished

The kitchen finish should feel intentional, with sink areas left clean, dry, and presentation-ready.

03

Straight vacuum lines

Floors are finished with visible order, especially in bedrooms, living areas, rugs, and high-traffic spaces.

04

Bathrooms presentation-ready

Mirrors, fixtures, vanities, toilets, tubs, and showers should look ready for guests, not merely wiped down.

05

Communication before arrival

Access, timing, service type, priorities, and expectations are clarified before the team begins.

06

Final walkthrough mindset

The visible finish is reviewed before completion so the client is not left managing loose ends.

The home should smell freshly cleaned, never overpowering. The goal is calm, polished, and cared for.

How much does house cleaning cost?
Maintenance cleanings may start around $180. Deep cleanings usually start around $450+ depending on size, condition, service type, add-ons, and frequency.
Do I need to be home?
No. Many clients provide entry instructions. We confirm access and priorities before service day.
Do you bring supplies?
Yes. Purelux brings professional supplies and equipment unless you request specific products in advance.
What is included in a deep cleaning?
Kitchens, bathrooms, buildup, baseboards, fixtures, floors, touchpoints, and detail zones that need a stronger reset.
