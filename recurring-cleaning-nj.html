BEFORE YOU REQUEST

A few useful answers.

Do you offer weekly and bi-weekly recurring cleaning?+

Yes. Purelux Cleaning offers weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly recurring house cleaning across North NJ, depending on availability and home needs.

Do I need a deep cleaning before recurring service starts?+

If the home needs a reset, we may recommend deep cleaning first. This helps recurring maintenance start from a cleaner and more manageable baseline.

What rooms are covered during recurring cleaning?+

Recurring cleaning commonly includes kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, surfaces, dusting, floors, touchpoints, and agreed priority zones.

Can I change my frequency later?+

Yes. Frequency can be reviewed as the home changes, whether you need more support, less support, or seasonal adjustments.
